Your eight habits

    Personal-day boundary

    Your personal day does not end at midnight. Anything you record before this time belongs to the previous personal day — handy after a late shift.

    Changing this never moves records you already saved. It only changes which date counts as “today”.

    Work schedule

    Each schedule applies from its start date. If two schedules cover the same day, the one that starts later is used. Changing a schedule never changes your saved habits.

    Backup & restore

    Your records live in this browser only (localStorage). They do not sync between devices, and clearing your browser data can erase them — so download a backup now and then.
